## Direction contract

THESIS: Tracinhos is a party-game with a code, executed at Kahoot craft: oversized actions, unmissable turn, living nick list. It refuses the timid dark-card stack and refuses school/notebook costume.

OWN-WORLD: Night living-room phones. Navy field, paper-white board, electric yellow primary, player colors as fat pads. Sora for UI, Anton for the shout (wordmark, code, SUA VEZ). No nested cards.

STORY: You sit down, pick nick and color, create or join, wait as names appear, then the board is the table. Success is starting a match without hunting the button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width wordmark in the header. Lede is the product sentence. Criar sala owns the stage (grid, nick, 8 pads, giant Criar). Entrar is the second beat with a 4-character code field. Regras is a text link, not a card. In play: SUA VEZ / Vez de X at shout size beside a large seconds timer; score rows as pills; paper board filling the rest.

FORM: canon / category standard. Seed key d2ae0cdf. Quality bar: Kahoot.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
